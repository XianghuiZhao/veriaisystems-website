5. Your Rights (GDPR)

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have the following rights regarding any personal data that may be held:

  • Right of access (Art. 15 GDPR)
  • Right to rectification (Art. 16 GDPR)
  • Right to erasure (Art. 17 GDPR)
  • Right to restriction of processing (Art. 18 GDPR)
  • Right to data portability (Art. 20 GDPR)

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at info@veriai-systems.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with a competent supervisory authority. In Germany, the responsible authority depends on the federal state of the data controller. The federal supervisory authority is the Bundesbeauftragter für den Datenschutz und die Informationsfreiheit (BfDI).
